## Focus

A company case as the starting point for ideation around an AI-driven application.

## What We Worked On

- worked on a project idea proposal
- used a concrete business context as the starting point
- identified problem areas where AI could become relevant

## Key Concepts

The published material for this day was brief, but the direction was clear: the project idea had to be anchored in a real business context rather than in an artificial demo task.

That created a focus on connecting technical possibilities with concrete workflows, services, and information needs.

## Practical Notes

The linked company material gave a public-facing picture of a business domain involving hospitality, events, stays, and communication. It is a natural starting point for considering solutions related to information access, content structuring, or assisted communication, but no formal requirement specification had been published.

## Key Takeaways

- a good AI idea starts with a clearly defined problem area
- the business context sets the frame for the value of the system
- without a clear case, it is difficult to judge whether AI is relevant
