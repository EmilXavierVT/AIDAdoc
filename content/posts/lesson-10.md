## Focus

Collaboration with agentic development tools.

## What We Worked On

- treated collaboration as a topic in its own right within agentic development
- prepared for a development model where multiple actors share responsibility for the same solution

## Key Concepts

The published material for this topic was very limited and only described the theme at a high level. The academic focus was therefore that development with agents is not only about individual prompts, but also about collaboration, coordination, and division of work.

## Key Takeaways

- agentic development is also a collaboration problem
- role distribution and clear workflows become more important as more agents and people are involved
- detailed materials and assignments had not yet been published for this topic

## Reflection

This lesson made collaboration feel like a technical topic, not only a team habit. When people and agents work in the same codebase, the workflow needs clear ownership, communication, and review. In my project work, that means being more explicit about what belongs in the backend, what belongs in the frontend, and what needs to be documented so the next person can continue without guessing.

## Project Connection

The project is split across backend and frontend work, so collaboration depends on clear API expectations and shared responsibility for the user flow.
