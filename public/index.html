Lesson 02: RAG I

Focus Introduction to retrieval-augmented generation and the difference between a standard chatbot and a solution that works with its own documents. What We Worked On worked with RAG as an architecture compared different platforms for document-based chatbots used source documents as the data foundation evaluated quality, flexibility, and usability Key Concepts RAG was treated as a way to connect a language model to a limited source foundation. The point is that answers should be grounded in actual sources rather than only in the model’s general knowledge.
